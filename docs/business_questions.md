# Business questions and analysis approach

## Sales
Compare monthly gross sales, completed orders and average order value. Separate order-volume changes from basket-size changes.

## Menu performance
Rank items by revenue, units and estimated contribution. High revenue does not automatically mean high profit; validate recipe costs and waste.

## Demand planning
Use weekday/hour order counts to identify busy windows. Synthetic timestamps are for demonstration, not staffing decisions.

## Customer behavior
Calculate repeat customer share and order frequency using completed orders. Apply privacy safeguards before exposing customer-level detail.

## Data improvements
Add an inventory transaction ledger, recipe ingredients and quantities, discounts/refunds, taxes, delivery fees and labor costs. Reconcile sales with the POS system before presenting financial performance.
